# Surface brief: home page hero

Scope and mode: Persuade, section of the established landing page `/`, redesign of the first viewport and the strip below it inside Pathly's world.
Audience and job: school leavers and graduates in Kazakhstan deciding whether Pathly is for them. Action: Get started (profile). Proof available: the real mechanism with illustrative data (requirement, source, tier, date, status); no invented customers or numbers.
Constraints: palette and Onest fixed; copy claims stay honest; no stock photos of people.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hero is a printed admissions bulletin: requirements set on dot leaders with a verification stamp per line, so the visitor sees the mechanism instead of reading about it. Refuses the headline-plus-screenshot-card split with badge pills and the row of equal icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Warm whites with navy ink in Onest, blue for the action, status colours only on the stamps; dot-leader rows like a table of contents, one round "verified source" seal, hairline rules and a route line that reappears in the section below. Recognizable with the words removed as a ruled printed list with stamps on the right.
STORY: The visitor understands in one glance that every requirement carries a source and a date and that an unconfirmed one is said to be unknown; believes it because the example line shows exactly that; clicks Get started.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440x900 inside the sheet. Headline "Know where you stand and what to do next." very large (about 88px, tight) across the left two thirds with a short paragraph and the blue Get started pill plus a quiet text link to browse programmes; across the lower two thirds a full-width bulletin sheet titled with the programme, six dot-leader rows each ending in a status stamp with source, tier and date beneath, a round seal overlapping the top-right corner, and one result line saying how many are met and that eligibility cannot be stated while one is unknown.
FORM: Printed bulletin with dot leaders, first of five hero structures considered for the section, no concept roll: a local section inside the established Pathly surface inherits its world (new-work: no concept tournament for a local section), so there is no seed reference
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
